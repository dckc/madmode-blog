> Collected while outlining "llm scrapers - outline.md" (Oct. 2026). Personal history with Aaron Swartz, with sources.
> 🙋 = link Claude's fetcher couldn't read; ❓ = for me to fill in.

1. I knew Aaron from when he was 14
	1. how we met: the W3C RDF Core Working Group; its Sebastopol face-to-face (Aug. 1, 2001) has us both in the room, Aaron joining IRC as "Aaron-F2F"[^rdfcore-f2f]
		1. ❓ first meeting in person? I recall Aaron blogging about it; not found yet (his numbered weblog starts in 2002) 🙋 [aaronsw.com/weblog](http://www.aaronsw.com/weblog/)
	2. he blew my mind about copyright in 2002, on IRC
		1. me: "if I can play my song without a license, what motivates me to acquire a license... or to compensate the artist in any way?"
		2. Aaron: "you pay because you want the artist to live well and continue making music"[^rdfig-2002] (A Return to Patronism, [2010](https://www.madmode.com/2010/06/return-to-patronism.html))
		3. → the copyright bind (llm scrapers outline, IX.4)
		4. his "Bake, Don't Fry" is why this blog is static files (MadMode goes from fried to baked, [2012](https://www.madmode.com/2012/bake-fry-frozen-flask.html))
		5. relaying his viewpoint at XML 2005 ([2005](https://www.madmode.com/2005/breadcrumbs_0023.html)); in person at the 'tute ([2006](https://www.madmode.com/2006/breadcrumbs_0133.html))
	3. the learning went both ways: he dedicated *A Programmable Web* "To Dan Connolly, who not only created the Web but found time to teach it to me"[^programmable-web]; "I always find myself much more inclined to work after talking to Paul Graham or Dan Connolly"[^aaronsw-productive]
